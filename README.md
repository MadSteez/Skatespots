# SPOTS

A shared map of skate spots — browse them, search or filter by obstacle
type, and add your own with photos and a description. Works the same way
on your phone and on a computer.

## Getting around

- **Map view** shows every spot as a pin. Tap a pin to open that spot.
- **List view** shows the same spots as cards with a name, description,
  and thumbnail, so you can scan through them quickly.
- On a phone, switch between the two with the **Map / List** tabs at the
  bottom of the screen. On a bigger screen, both are visible side by side.
- Tap the **locate button** (the crosshair, bottom-right of the map) to
  center the map on where you are.

## Finding a spot

- Type into the **search bar** at the top to match a spot's name or
  description.
- Tap the **tag chips** just below the search bar (ledge, stairs, rail,
  gap, etc.) to filter by obstacle type. Tap a tag again to remove it, or
  hit **Clear filters** to reset everything.
- Search and tag filters combine, and apply to both the map and the list
  at the same time.

## Opening a spot

Tap a spot (from a pin or a list card) to see its full details: all of its
photos, the description, its tags, and its exact coordinates. Tap any
photo there to see it full-size — tap it again or hit the × to close.

## Adding a spot

1. Tap the **+ Add spot** button.
2. Give it a name and, optionally, a description — ground conditions,
   security, best time to go, that kind of thing.
3. Set its location either by typing coordinates directly or by hitting
   **Pick on map** and tapping the exact spot.
4. Add tags for the type of obstacle (there are suggested tags you can
   tap to add quickly, or type your own, comma-separated).
5. Add up to 10 photos if you have them — not required, but helpful for
   everyone else trying to find the place.
6. Hit **Save spot**.

Adding, editing, or deleting a spot requires a personal access token —
ask whoever manages this page to set you up with one. Without a token you
can still see and search every spot, you just can't change the list. If
you have a token, paste it into **Setup & sync** (the gear icon) once and
it's remembered on that device.

## Editing or deleting a spot

Open the spot, then use the **Edit** or **Delete** buttons in its detail
view. Deleting asks you to confirm first, since it can't be undone.

## A few things worth knowing

- Everyone sees the same shared list — if you add a spot, your friends
  will see it too (it might take a few seconds to show up for them).
- Changing your own device's browser or clearing its data doesn't affect
  anyone else's view of the list.
- If a spot's location looks slightly off, you can always edit it and
  re-pick the location on the map.
